Interconnection
February 13, 2022 By Mohammed Taher Al-Hamdani

An Example of the Science of Interconnection

← Back to Articles
Originally published on Mafaateeh Al-Hamdani | The Veteran blog
The spiral of the science of interconnection.

The science of interconnection — only for voracious readers.

She went to have lunch as if nothing had happened.

Successive shocks affect one's feelings; a person begins to not care, and doesn't bother whether the tea is hot or cold, with or without sugar — so one must be cautious in such matters. True, when you strip yourself of emotions you naturally become stronger, but humans — especially the fragile vessels — if they are stripped of their feelings, their cruelty splits stone and scatters trees like a thunderbolt from the farthest sky to which al-Buraq ascended.

We spent hours in our conversation, yet it passed like minutes.

What is she to us, and us to her? What are these hollow, empty relationships? How do people live like this? Yes, they can fill them with children and their dependents — "wealth and children are the adornment of worldly life, and the everlasting good deeds are better with your Lord" — yes, better with your Lord — so we must worship Him until certainty comes to us. Did we know what worship is, or what certainty is? No, we did not know, and I do not know it to delve into it.

Did we understand the meaning of love, or was Qays, the madman of Layla, more knowledgeable about love?

First, love must elevate us to love of the Great, the Most Magnificent?

Why do I hear talks and sayings about women searching for a savior from the horrors of war? And since when was children's obedience to fathers in marriage an obedience? But the word has an effect — its name is "in-law ties" (muṣāhara), and iron is only melted when heated to the highest degree.

Will the war end, or will it continue as the black decade continued in the country of a million martyrs against the country of perfumes, whose women still do not bathe properly?

And I speak of war: regional states think the peoples will revolt. But how can the people of wisdom revolt while drugged with qat? Even if they revolt, a revolution without leadership to lead it, run it, and plan for it is of no use, and its tragedy on the peoples will be greater.

I am amazed at those who want to travel without setting a specific destination, like one who wants a car without knowing the specifications he wants.

Sail in your world, for as the conqueror of Khaybar and carrier of its gate said: "You think you are a small body, yet the greater world is folded within you."

Forgive me if I merged more than one topic at once — that is my writing style in my articles; I like them to be spiral. If women laid them down, street children would lighten, but sex is the cheapest entertainment for states whose peoples refuse to develop, due to a false belief. Everyone condemns and praises secular Turkey, which rose from the decrepitude of the sick man's inheritance.

We Yemenis must rise, help one another, and form blocs whose goal and aim is the reform of Yemen. Forgive me again — I got excited in my writing and my patriotism started to get out of hand, so pay it no attention. And I still believe that the impact of Elon Musk and his satellites on Yemen will be more beneficial than all the organizations working in Yemen combined. If you want to reform Yemen, start by installing a receiver for satellite waves with a solar battery, and the internet will take care of educating the people. Yes, let them open porn sites and let them be shocked — afterwards they will realize the importance and benefit of the internet, and with proper guidance they will start benefiting from it. And remember my words: Yemen Net will offer better deals and bigger capacities, and they will work better on developing the maritime internet line — but only after the destruction of Malta, whose language somewhat resembles Arabic.

If you have reached this point, it means you are a reader. If you would like to participate in printing an illustrated book into Arabic, put your name in the comments. If you have experience in website design, let us know and ease the price for us, friend. And if you have a radio voice like broadcaster Safaa Ghanem, let us know, to see whether you have the ability to record a book and turn it into an audiobook, upload it on the site, sell it, and make its proceeds go to developing Yemen and raising a conscious generation that knows where it is going and what it will become, engraving high and sublime morals in them from childhood.

Thank you for reading — I wanted to share with you part of my dreams that I wish to see one day in this fleeting life.

Stay in God's protection and care.
