Tawzan: A Hamdani Village in Bani Makram, Sana'a Governorate

Summary

Tawzan is a Yemeni village in Hamdan District, Sana'a Governorate, part of Bani Makram sub-district.

Administrative Location

  • Yemen
  • Sana'a Governorate
  • Hamdan District

  • Tawzan

Population
