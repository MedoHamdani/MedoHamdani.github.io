Flat Feet: A Complete Guide

Causes, symptoms, exercises, orthotics, and practical treatment options for flat feet (pes planus).

Healthy arch structure vs flattened foot

What Are Flat Feet?

Flat feet, or pes planus, occur when the arch of the foot collapses and the sole touches the ground fully. This can cause foot pain, knee pain, back pain, and reduced mobility.

Causes

  • Genetics and family history
  • Foot or ankle injuries
  • Aging and tissue weakening
  • Obesity and excess weight
  • Weak foot muscles

Symptoms

  • Pain in the foot, knee, or lower back
  • Swelling along the inner ankle
  • Difficulty standing on tiptoes
  • Shoes wearing out unevenly

Best Exercises

Research shows targeted exercises can improve arch support and reduce pain. Key methods include towel scrunches, marble pickups, toe separators, tibialis raises, and calf raises.

Strengthening both intrinsic and extrinsic foot muscles is important for long-term improvement.

Treatment Options

  1. Foot exercises and stretching
  2. Orthotics and supportive footwear
  3. Physical therapy
  4. Weight management
  5. Activity modification and rest
