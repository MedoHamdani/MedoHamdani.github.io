🗣️ The Three-Phase System

Marriage is a serious commitment — half of your Deen. To avoid the confusion of endless messaging and ghosting, we follow a structured, three-phase system designed to help couples build genuine clarity before committing.

Phase 1 — Compatibility Check

Initial conversation to assess basic compatibility, expectations, and mutual interest.

  • Discuss life goals and family plans
  • Clarify timelines for marriage
  • Introduce families to the process

Phase 2 — Values & Finances

Deep-dive into values, responsibilities, and financial planning.

  • Bills and budgeting
  • Parenting styles
  • Debt and credit history
  • Religious practice and expectations
  • Approach to family relationships

Phase 3 — Long-term Vision

Final phase — build the future together before saying "yes."

  • Childhood traumas / healing
  • Sexual expectations
  • Dream home and bucket list
  • Careers, education, political views
  • Family health and mental health history
  • Financial expectations

💡 Why this system? Love alone is not enough. Respect, trust, and mutual understanding form the real foundation of a successful marriage. A plan helps both parties enter with clear eyes and honest hearts.
