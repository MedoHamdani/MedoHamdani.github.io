Profile Template

Please include as much as you can. If unsure, follow this template:

  • Hobbies
  • About yourself: Height, Weight (optional), Blood type (optional)
  • Personality Type: e.g. INTJ (16-personality test)
  • Location History: How long in Australia / born here?
  • Languages Spoken
  • Relocation: Open to moving?

The Three-Phase System

Marriage is a serious commitment — half of your Deen. To avoid confusion and ghosting, we follow a structured three-phase system.

Phase 1 — Compatibility Check

  • Life goals, family plans
  • Marriage timeline
  • Introduce families to the process

Phase 2 — Values & Finances

  • Bills and budgeting
  • Parenting styles
  • Debt and credit history
  • Religious practice and expectations
  • Approach to family relationships

Phase 3 — Long-term Vision

  • Childhood traumas / healing
  • Sexual expectations
  • Dream home and bucket list
  • Careers, education, political views
  • Family health and mental health history
  • Financial expectations

Why this system? Love alone is not enough. Respect, trust, and mutual understanding are essential. A plan helps both parties enter with clear eyes and honest hearts.
